Mehmet Korkmaz

Product Engineer @reformo.
Coding since '97 but I'm neither a ninja nor guru nor junkie.

“Everything we hear is an opinion, not a fact. Everything we see is a perspective, not the truth.”
~ Marcus Aurelius ~

I am a product engineer with a strong backend and architecture background. For more than two decades, I have combined hands-on software development with technical leadership, helping turn business needs into working products. I care about understanding the problem, deciding what is worth building, and making the engineering choices that move the product forward.

My work spans mobility and IoT platforms, financial analytics, gaming and community products, and B2B applications. I have built multi-tenant backends, worked on financial reporting systems, and connected products with payment providers, accounting software, and operational services. My experience also includes leading development teams, guiding architectural transitions, and bringing products to launch.

I approach product development by identifying the most valuable problem to solve, delivering in small increments, and using feedback to shape the next step. Understanding business rules and operational constraints is part of my engineering work. I aim to make scope, risks, and trade-offs explicit so that technical decisions support the product’s priorities and the team can sustain its pace.

I use AI-assisted development as part of my engineering workflow, working with coding agents to explore codebases, develop features, and document how systems work. I bring the product context and architectural intent to that collaboration, and treat generated output as something to inspect, test, and refine. The goal is to shorten the path from an idea to a useful, verified result while retaining responsibility for the decisions and the software delivered.

My architectural approach emphasizes clear domain boundaries, explicit contracts, and testable business logic. I want systems to remain understandable and adaptable as products evolve, with complexity justified by the problem at hand. Whether writing code, shaping a feature, or guiding a team through a technical decision, I stay close to implementation while keeping the broader product in view.
